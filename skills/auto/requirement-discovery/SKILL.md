---
name: requirement-discovery
description: Use this skill when starting a new task to identify and verify requirements.
---
1. Review the task description and any provided documentation thoroughly.
2. Identify key outputs and their expected formats.
3. List any rules or constraints mentioned, such as naming conventions or data types.
4. Check for examples or templates that illustrate the desired output.
5. Validate the requirements with stakeholders or through additional research if necessary.
6. Document the requirements clearly for future reference.
